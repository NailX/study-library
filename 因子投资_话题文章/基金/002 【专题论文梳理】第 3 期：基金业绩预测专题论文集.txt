这是专题论文梳理的第 3 期推文。本期我们转向基金业绩的预测因子。我们依然挑出了 10 篇经典的相关文献。如同在上一篇研究文章【063】可复制的因子研究中提到的，后续我们会陆续补充基金研究数据，届时会陆续增加这些基金业绩预测指标数据。与此前的异象研究不同，关于基金业绩预测指标的研究，不少发表在 RFS ，JF 上也有较多研究，而 JFE 上的经典研究则相对较少。
此外，在知乎版本推文中，点击文章题目，便可以直接进入文章官方主页。[编号] 01[题目] On persistence in mutual fund performance[作者] Mark M. Carhart[期刊] Journal of Finance[年份/卷数] 1997, 52 (1)[摘要] Using a sample free of survivor bias, I demonstrate that common factors in stock returns and investment expenses almost completely explain persistence in equity mutual funds' mean and risk‐adjusted returns. Hendricks, Patel and Zeckhauser's (1993) “hot hands” result is mostly driven by the one‐year momentum effect of Jegadeesh and Titman (1993), but individual funds do not earn higher returns from following the momentum strategy in stocks. The only significant persistence not explained is concentrated in strong underperformance by the worst‐return mutual funds. The results do not support the existence of skilled or informed mutual fund portfolio managers.[核心贡献] 在指出动量因子有助于解释基金业绩的同时，指出 1 年期 Carhart 四因子模型 alpha 可以预测基金的未来表现。[引用数] 16934（截至 2020/07/30）
[编号] 02[题目] How active is your fund manager? A new measure that predicts performance[作者] K J Martin Cremers, Antti Petajisto[期刊] Review of Financial Studies[年份/卷数] 2009, 22 (9)[摘要] We introduce a new measure of active portfolio management, Active Share, which represents the share of portfolio holdings that differ from the benchmark index holdings. We compute Active Share for domestic equity mutual funds from 1980 to 2003. We relate Active Share to fund characteristics such as size, expenses, and turnover in the cross-section, and we also examine its evolution over time. Active Share predicts fund performance: funds with the highest Active Share significantly outperform their benchmarks, both before and after expenses, and they exhibit strong performance persistence. Nonindex funds with the lowest Active Share underperform their benchmarks.[核心贡献] 指出 active share（基金持仓相对于业绩基准的偏离幅度）同基金未来业绩显著正相关。[引用数]1524（截至 2020/07/30）
[编号] 03[题目] Judging fund managers by the company they keep[作者] Randolph B. Cohen, Joshua D. Coval, Lubos Pastor[期刊] Journal of Finance[年份/卷数] 2005, 60 (3)[摘要] We develop a performance evaluation approach in which a fund manager's skill is judged by the extent to which the manager's investment decisions resemble the decisions of managers with distinguished performance records. The proposed performance measures use historical returns and holdings of many funds to evaluate the performance of a single fund. Simulations demonstrate that our measures are particularly useful in ranking managers. In an application that relies on such ranking, our measures reveal strong predictability in the returns of U.S. equity funds. Our measures provide information about future fund returns that is not contained in the standard measures.[核心贡献] 他们基于基金经理能力和基金股票持仓，通过迭代法构建了一个新指标，并指出该指标同基金未来业绩显著正相关。具体而言，首先依据基金持有一支特定股票的权重对基金能力加权得到该股票的质量指标，再依据一支基金的持仓对其持股的质量加权得到基金加权能力指标。[引用数] 331（截至 2020/07/30）
[编号] 04[题目] A flow-based explanation for return predictability[作者] Dong Lou[期刊] Review of Financial Studies[年份/卷数] 2012, 23 (1)[摘要] I propose and test a capital-flow-based explanation for some well-known empirical regularities concerning return predictability—the persistence of mutual fund performance, the “smart money” effect, and stock price momentum. First, I construct a measure of demand shocks to individual stocks by aggregating flow-induced trading across all mutual funds, and document a significant, temporary price impact of such uninformed trading. Next, given that mutual fund flows are highly predictable, I show that the expected part of flow-induced trading positively forecasts stock and mutual fund returns in the following year, which are then reversed in subsequent years. The main findings of the paper are that the flow-driven return effect can fully account for mutual fund performance persistence and the smart money effect, and can partially explain stock price momentum.[核心贡献] 构建了一个资金流驱动的交易指标，并指出预期资金流驱动的交易可以对股票和基金未来一年的业绩有显著为正的预测力。通过上述研究解释了基金中存在的聪明钱效应，并部分解释了股票动量。[引用数] 449（截至 2020/07/30）
[编号] 05[题目] Assessing the costs and benefits of brokers in the mutual fund industry[作者] Daniel Bergstresser, John M. R. Chalmers, Peter Tufano[期刊] Review of Financial Studies[年份/卷数] 2010, 22 (10)[摘要] Many investors purchase mutual funds through intermediated channels, paying brokers or financial advisors for fund selection and advice. This article attempts to quantify the benefits that investors enjoy in exchange for the costs of these services. We study broker-sold and direct-sold funds from 1996 to 2004, and fail to find that brokers deliver substantial tangible benefits. Relative to direct-sold funds, broker-sold funds deliver lower risk-adjusted returns, even before subtracting distribution costs. These results hold across fund objectives, with the exception of foreign equity funds. Further, broker-sold funds exhibit no more skill at aggregate-level asset allocation than do funds sold through the direct channel. Our results are consistent with two hypotheses: that brokers deliver substantial intangible benefits that we do not observe and that there are material conflicts of interest between brokers and their clients.[核心贡献] 发现相比直接销售的基金，通过经纪商销售的基金的风险调整后回报更低。这与经纪商可能可以为投资者带来显著的无形利益的假说相符。[引用数] 553（截至 2020/07/30）
[编号] 06[题目] Mutual Fund's as Predictor of Performance[作者] Yakov Amihud, Ruslan Goyenko[期刊] Review of Financial Studies[年份/卷数] 2013, 26 (3)[摘要] We propose that fund performance can be predicted by its , obtained from a regression of its returns on a multifactor benchmark model. Lower indicates greater selectivity, and it significantly predicts better performance. Stock funds sorted into lowest-quintile lagged and highest-quintile lagged alpha produce significant annual alpha of 3.8%. Across funds, is positively associated with fund size and negatively associated with its expenses and manager's tenure.[核心贡献] 指出基金收益对基准因子模型回归的 同基金未来业绩显著负相关， 越低的基金选股能力越强，未来业绩越好。[引用数] 441（截至 2020/07/30）
[编号] 07[题目] Unobserved actions of mutual funds[作者] Marcin Kacperczyk, Clemens Sialm, Lu Zheng[期刊] Review of Financial Studies[年份/卷数] 2008, 21 (6)[摘要] Despite extensive disclosure requirements, mutual fund investors do not observe all actions of fund managers. We estimate the impact of unobserved actions on fund returns using the return gap—the difference between the reported fund return and the return on a portfolio that invests in the previously disclosed fund holdings. We document that unobserved actions of some funds persistently create value, while such actions of other funds destroy value. Our main result shows that the return gap predicts fund performance.[核心贡献] 指出投资者不可直接观测的基金经理的高频交易行为带来的收益，对基金的未来业绩有显著为正的预测能力。[引用数] 713（截至 2020/07/30）
[编号] 08[题目] Time‐varying fund manager skill[作者] Marcin Kacperczyk, Stijn van Nieuwerburgh, Laura Veldkamp[期刊] Journal of Finance[年份/卷数] 2014, 69 (4)[摘要] We propose a new definition of skill as general cognitive ability to pick stocks or time the market. We find evidence for stock picking in booms and market timing in recessions. Moreover, the same fund managers that pick stocks well in expansions also time the market well in recessions. These fund managers significantly outperform other funds and passive benchmarks. Our results suggest a new measure of managerial ability that weighs a fund's market timing more in recessions and stock picking more in booms. The measure displays more persistence than either market timing or stock picking alone and predicts fund performance.[核心贡献] 指出基金经理的能力是时变的，在衰退期会更关注择时，而在扩张期会更关注选股。与此同时，那些在扩张期选股能力较强的基金经理，也在衰退期有更好择时能力。作者们据此构建了一个加权的时变能力指标，并指出该指标对基金未来业绩有显著为正的预测能力。[引用数] 433（截至 2020/07/30）
[编号] 09[题目] Fund manager use of public information: New evidence on managerial skills[作者] Marcin Kacperczyk, Amit Seru[期刊] Journal of Finance[年份/卷数] 2007, 62 (2)[摘要] We show theoretically that the responsiveness of a fund manager's portfolio allocations to changes in public information decreases in the manager's skill. We go on to estimate this sensitivity (RPI ) as the of the regression of changes in a manager's portfolio holdings on changes in public information using a panel of U.S. equity funds. Consistent with RPI containing information related to managerial skills, we find a strong inverse relationship between RPI and various existing measures of performance, and between RPI and fund flows. We also document that both fund‐ and manager‐specific attributes affect RPI .[核心贡献] 指出基金投资组合变化对公共信息的敏感程度（RPI）同基金未来业绩以及资金流显著负相关。[引用数] 373（截至 2020/07/30）
[编号] 10[题目] Risk shifting and mutual fund performance[作者] Jennifer Huang, Clemens Sialm, Hanjiang Zhang[期刊] Review of Financial Studies[年份/卷数] 2011, 24 (8)[摘要] Mutual funds change their risk levels significantly over time. Risk shifting might be caused by ill-motivated trades of unskilled or agency-prone fund managers who trade to increase their personal compensation. Alternatively, risk shifting might occur when skilled fund managers trade to take advantage of their stock selection and timing abilities. This article investigates the performance consequences of risk shifting and sheds light on the mechanisms and the economic motivations behind risk-shifting behavior. Using a holdings-based measure of risk shifting, we find that funds that increase risk perform worse than funds that keep stable risk levels over time, suggesting that risk shifting either is an indication of inferior ability or is motivated by agency issues.[核心贡献] 指出那些风险水平经常变化的基金的未来业绩显著低于风险水平较为稳定的基金。
